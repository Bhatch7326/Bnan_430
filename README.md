### Data Visualization in Power BI — DataCamp
**Completed:** October 12, 2026
**Certificate:** https://github.com/Bhatch7326/Bnan_430/blob/main/Certificates/Power%20BI%20Data%20VIz.pdf 
**Tableau Story:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/bryce.hatch/viz/BNAN430Portfolio/IntrotoPowerBI?publish=yes
My case rests on work I’ve done, not on tools I’ve opened. In Flex 4 I built the Account Portfolio Dashboard in Tableau. Its Top 10 Accounts view is a plain ranked table, so Anita and Marcus have to read every Net Contributions figure to spot an account that is slipping. If I rebuilt it in Power BI with conditional formatting, a red-to-green scale on Net Contributions would flag those accounts before anyone reads a number. In Flex 6, DataCamp’s Introduction to DAX in Power BI, I wrote measures with CALCULATE to change filter context. Applied here, a single Net Contributions measure could answer by tier, by account, or against last year.
At Southwest I would keep Excel for one-off reconciliations, quick ad hoc questions, and anything finance will audit cell by cell. Tableau fits exploratory analysis and presentation stories, where freedom of layout matters more than governance. Power BI fits reports that need a shared data model, scheduled refresh, and a home inside Microsoft 365. My honest gap is that all of my Power BI work has been DataCamp exercises on prepared data, and I have never pointed Power BI Desktop at real company data. I would close it by connecting Power BI Desktop to Southwest’s live account data and rebuilding the Account Portfolio Dashboard as a shared model. Before Anita and Marcus rely on it, I would check every total against the Excel figures finance already trusts.
